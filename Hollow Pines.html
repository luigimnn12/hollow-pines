Ranger Ortiz's field guide · Hollow Pines, 1987

What lives in the pines

In the summer of 1987 the Ashgrove Timber Company reopened the old Underpass to run trucks through the mountain. The crew broke into a cave that went down farther than their ropes. What came up learned the town by copying it: a face to watch them, ears to hear them, and the stick figures they built to keep it out. By August, Hollow Pines was empty.

The story

Chapter One · The Road Out. Sam's call came over the radio at 11:52 PM. You fell asleep at the wheel on Route 9 and woke on a dirt road in a forest that is not on any map. Ranger Ortiz's broadcast has been looping here since 1987. The thing in these woods learned to copy voices. You are starting to wonder who really called you.

Chapter Two · The Underpass. Under the mountain is the tunnel that started everything, and the cars of everyone who tried to escape through it. One of them might still run.

Ortiz's tapes. Five cassette tapes are hidden near the ranger station, the lookout tower, a campground and other places. Each one is a log Ortiz recorded in the summer of 1987. Find all five for the true ending.

The shrines. Three stick shrines in the woods are where the Effigy builds its bodies. Burn them with matches. Every shrine you burn keeps it away longer; burn all three and it is gone for the night.

Bottles. Throw a bottle and the Listener runs to where it shatters.

Mara Ashgrove, fourteen, hid her journal pages in the woods for whoever came next. Read them. They tell you what Ortiz learned too late.

What helps

  • Flashlight. The Watcher and the Effigy both flee from it. The Listener cannot see it at all.
  • Fire and flares. Nothing will cross a lit campfire or a burning flare.
  • Wardrobes. Hide if it didn't see you go in.
  • Crouching. Quiet enough to slip past the Listener.
  • The radio. Its signal bars rise when something is near.
